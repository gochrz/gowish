# Step one earnings copy design

## Goal

Explain that completing the GoWish setup is required for the $20 bonus and that the creator can continue earning commissions through their GoWish lists.

## Considered approaches

1. Add the message inside the existing white steps card. This keeps related content together but makes the card feel denser.
2. Add a highlighted callout above the steps. This draws attention but competes with the payment and email notices already on the page.
3. Add a short introductory paragraph beneath `Join GoWish`. This is the approved approach because it answers the question at the exact point shown while preserving the current visual hierarchy.

## Approved copy

`Complete the steps below to qualify for your $20 bonus. And the opportunity doesn’t stop there—once your account is active, you can continue earning commissions when people shop through your GoWish lists.`

## Presentation

- Place the paragraph directly beneath the `Step one / Join GoWish` heading and above the existing steps card.
- Use the existing body typeface and muted text color.
- Keep the paragraph compact and aligned with the current content width.
- Do not add a new card, background, icon, animation, or page section.

## Verification

- A static test must confirm the exact copy and its position between the heading and steps card.
- All existing tests and type checks must pass.
- Desktop and mobile views must preserve the current rhythm without overflow.
- The public site must show the exact deployed revision without browser errors.
