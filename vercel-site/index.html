    Background

    What GoWish actually is

    GoWish is a wishlist and social shopping app. Creators build lists of products they would recommend anyway, share one link, and earn commission every time a follower buys through it. The lists are permanent, so unlike a story link it keeps earning after 24 hours.

    The Creator Universe works with 75,000+ brands including Nike, Sephora, Lululemon, Skims, UGG and Target. Onboarding is fast, usually inside a day.

    Whatever you earn on GoWish is separate from our bonus. You keep both.

    Background

    When you actually get paid

    GoWish pays us for creators they approve. We pay you out of that.

    So the sequence is: you get approved, GoWish counts you in their next settlement, that money reaches us, and we send your $20 through your preferred payout method. We are not sitting on it, we just cannot send money we have not received yet.

    Approval is GoWish's call, not ours, and we pay on approved accounts only. If your approval is confirmed and a cycle passes without payment, keep your reference available so the payment can be reviewed.

    We are an independent partner of GoWish. We are not GoWish, we do not decide who gets approved, and the bonus is paid by us and not by GoWish.

    How it works

    Four steps

    1. Register below

      Name, email, payout details. Takes a minute. We issue your code immediately on screen.

    2. Send your creators your link

      Your code is baked into a link we give you, so your creators do not have to type anything. The link goes to the public creator page, not to this one.

    3. They sign up on GoWish and get approved

      They download GoWish, apply to the Creator Program, connect socials and finish their profile. GoWish approves inside 24 to 72 hours in most cases. Approval is GoWish's decision, not ours.

    4. You get paid

      We confirm approvals with GoWish, then send you $10 per approved creator through your preferred payout method.

    The honest version of the deal. GoWish pays us per approved creator. We pass a share to you and a share to the creator, and keep the rest. That is the whole arrangement, and it is why payment is tied to approvals rather than sign-ups.
